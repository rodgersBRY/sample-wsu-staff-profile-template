Biography

I am a Software Developer at Washington State University Global Health Kenya, with over five years of experience in software development, specializing in web and mobile applications, backend systems, and digital solutions.

My professional experience spans the design, development, deployment, and maintenance of scalable software systems, with expertise in modern web technologies, mobile application development, database management, and systems integration.

In my current role, I contribute to the development and enhancement of digital health and surveillance systems, including the Kenya Animal Biosurveillance System (KABS), supporting disease surveillance, data collection, reporting, and informed decision-making.
