Biography

I am a medical epidemiologist and global health researcher with over 15 years of experience in clinical medicine, public health, epidemiology, and research. I currently serve as a Medical Epidemiologist with Washington State University's Global Health Program in Kenya, providing technical and strategic leadership to research and public health surveillance programmes.

My expertise includes infectious disease epidemiology, population-based surveillance, emerging and zoonotic diseases, outbreak preparedness and response, One Health, and evidence-informed public health.

I previously served as Program Director of the NIH-funded Centers for Research in Emerging Infectious Diseases–East and Central Africa (CREID-ECA), leading multidisciplinary research programmes across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and the Democratic Republic of Congo. I have also worked with Kenya's Ministry of Health and served in medical leadership roles in Kakamega County.

My research includes Ebola, Rift Valley fever, SARS-CoV-2, respiratory diseases, and population-based surveillance. I am committed to translating research evidence into stronger public health systems and better health outcomes across Africa.
